Formula

The calculation used by this tool is:

Inventory ROI = (Gross Profit ÷ Average Inventory) × 100

Inventory ROI measures how much gross profit is generated relative to the average amount of capital tied up in inventory.

Example

A company earns $100,000 in gross profit while maintaining $250,000 of average inventory.

Inventory ROI:

($100,000 ÷ $250,000) × 100 = 40%

Therefore, the company's inventory ROI is 40%.

Actual performance can differ because of taxes, financing, depreciation, working capital, maintenance, attribution and other factors not included in a simple ROI model.

Frequently asked questions

What is ROI?

ROI, or return on investment, measures the return generated relative to the investment or cost used to generate it.

How is this calculator calculated?

Inventory ROI is calculated by dividing gross profit by average inventory investment and multiplying by 100.

Can ROI be negative?

Gross-profit-based inventory ROI is normally non-negative when gross profit is non-negative. A negative input would indicate a gross loss rather than gross profit.

What should I include?

Use gross profit for the selected period and the average inventory investment held during the same period.

Is ROI the same as profit?

No. Gross profit is an absolute monetary amount, while inventory ROI expresses gross profit relative to the average inventory investment.
