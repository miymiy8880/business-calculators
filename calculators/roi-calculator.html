Formula

The calculation used by this tool is:

ROI = ((Final Return − Investment Cost) ÷ Investment Cost) × 100

Net return is calculated as:

Net Return = Final Return − Investment Cost

ROI expresses the net return as a percentage of the original investment cost.

Example

An investment of $10,000 produces a final return of $15,000.

Net return: $15,000 − $10,000 = $5,000

ROI: (($15,000 − $10,000) ÷ $10,000) × 100 = 50%

Therefore, the investment has a net return of $5,000 and an ROI of 50%.

Actual performance can differ because of taxes, financing, depreciation, working capital, maintenance, attribution and other factors not included in a simple ROI model.

Frequently asked questions

What is ROI?

ROI, or return on investment, measures the gain or loss generated by an investment relative to its cost.

How is this calculator calculated?

ROI is calculated by subtracting investment cost from final return, dividing the result by investment cost, and multiplying by 100.

Can ROI be negative?

Yes. A negative ROI means the final return is lower than the original investment cost.

What should I include?

Include the total investment cost and the final monetary return generated by the investment.

Is ROI the same as profit?

No. Net return or profit is an absolute monetary amount, while ROI expresses that return as a percentage of the original investment cost.
