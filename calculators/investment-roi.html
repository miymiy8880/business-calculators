Formula

The calculation used by this tool is:

ROI = ((Final Value + Income − Initial Investment) ÷ Initial Investment) × 100

This calculation measures the total gain from the investment, including both the change in value and income received, relative to the original investment.

Example

An investment of $10,000 reaches $12,500 and produces $500 of income.

Total gain: $12,500 + $500 − $10,000 = $3,000

Investment ROI: ($3,000 ÷ $10,000) × 100 = 30%

Therefore, the investment produces a total ROI of 30%.

Actual performance can differ because of taxes, financing, depreciation, working capital, maintenance, attribution and other factors not included in a simple ROI model.

Frequently asked questions

What is ROI?

ROI, or return on investment, measures the gain or loss generated by an investment relative to the original investment.

How is this calculator calculated?

Investment ROI is calculated by adding final value and income received, subtracting the initial investment, dividing by the initial investment, and multiplying by 100.

Can ROI be negative?

Yes. A negative ROI means the final value plus income received is lower than the initial investment.

What should I include?

Use the original amount invested, the final value of the investment, and income received during the investment period.

Is ROI the same as profit?

No. Profit or gain is an absolute monetary amount, while ROI expresses that gain relative to the original investment.
