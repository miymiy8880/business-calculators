Formula

The calculation used by this tool is:

ROI = ((Annual Benefit − Investment) ÷ Investment) × 100

Where annual benefit is the sum of annual labor savings, annual operating savings and annual additional profit.

Annual Benefit = Labor Savings + Operating Savings + Additional Profit

Example

A $200,000 project produces $60,000 in annual labor savings, $30,000 in annual operating savings and $20,000 in annual additional profit.

Annual benefit: $60,000 + $30,000 + $20,000 = $110,000

Warehouse ROI: (($110,000 − $200,000) ÷ $200,000) × 100 = −45%

Under this simple first-year ROI model, the project produces a −45% ROI because its first-year benefits do not fully recover the initial investment.

Actual performance can differ because of taxes, financing, depreciation, working capital, maintenance, attribution and other factors not included in a simple ROI model.

Frequently asked questions

What is ROI?

ROI, or return on investment, measures the return generated relative to the investment required.

How is this calculator calculated?

Warehouse ROI is calculated by adding annual labor savings, annual operating savings and annual additional profit to determine annual benefit. The investment is then subtracted from annual benefit, divided by the investment and multiplied by 100.

Can ROI be negative?

Yes. A negative ROI means the annual benefits do not recover the warehouse investment under the assumptions used.

What should I include?

Include the warehouse investment and realistic annual labor savings, operating savings and additional profit attributable to the project.

Is ROI the same as profit?

No. Profit or savings are absolute monetary amounts, while ROI expresses the net benefit relative to the original investment.
