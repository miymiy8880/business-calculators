Formula

The calculation used by this tool is:

ROI = ((Annual Profit + Annual Savings − Machine Cost) ÷ Machine Cost) × 100

The calculation compares the annual financial benefits generated by the machine with its purchase cost.

Example

A $100,000 machine creates $40,000 in additional annual profit and $30,000 in annual savings.

Annual benefit: $40,000 + $30,000 = $70,000

Machine ROI: (($70,000 − $100,000) ÷ $100,000) × 100 = −30%

Under this simple first-year ROI model, the machine produces a −30% ROI because its first-year benefits do not fully recover its purchase cost.

Actual performance can differ because of taxes, financing, depreciation, working capital, maintenance, attribution and other factors not included in a simple ROI model.

Frequently asked questions

What is ROI?

ROI, or return on investment, measures the return generated relative to the cost of an investment.

How is this calculator calculated?

Machine ROI is calculated by adding annual additional profit and annual labor or operating savings, subtracting machine cost, dividing by machine cost, and multiplying by 100.

Can ROI be negative?

Yes. A negative ROI means the annual benefits do not recover the machine cost under the assumptions used.

What should I include?

Include the machine purchase cost and realistic annual additional profit and labor or operating savings attributable to the machine.

Is ROI the same as profit?

No. Profit is an absolute monetary amount, while ROI expresses the return relative to the machine investment.
