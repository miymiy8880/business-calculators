Formula

The calculation used by this tool is:

Marketing ROI = ((Attributable Return − Marketing Cost) ÷ Marketing Cost) × 100

The calculation measures the net return from a marketing campaign relative to the cost of the campaign.

Example

A campaign costs $10,000 and generates $25,000 of attributable return.

Net return: $25,000 − $10,000 = $15,000

Marketing ROI: (($25,000 − $10,000) ÷ $10,000) × 100 = 150%

Therefore, the campaign has a simple ROI of 150%.

Actual performance can differ because of taxes, financing, depreciation, working capital, maintenance, attribution and other factors not included in a simple ROI model.

Frequently asked questions

What is ROI?

ROI, or return on investment, measures the return generated relative to the cost of an investment.

How is this calculator calculated?

Marketing ROI is calculated by subtracting marketing cost from attributable return, dividing by marketing cost, and multiplying by 100.

Can ROI be negative?

Yes. A negative marketing ROI means the attributable return is lower than the marketing cost.

What should I include?

Include the total marketing cost for the campaign and the return that can reasonably be attributed to that campaign.

Is ROI the same as profit?

No. Profit or return is an absolute monetary amount, while ROI expresses the return relative to the marketing cost.
